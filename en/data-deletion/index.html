Game saves are mainly stored on your device. Below, find out what you can delete yourself and what you can request by email.

This guidance covers the currently released Google Play edition. It does not rely on cloud backups or cross-device restoration. The developer cannot remotely erase or restore saves on your device. Guidance for other editions will be added when they are released.

DataHow to delete it
