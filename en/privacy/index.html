Kidori's Delivery Express

Privacy policy — Kidori's Delivery Express

Information stored on your device

The current Google Play edition stores game progress and a random device-local identifier in Android app storage. Settings such as language are also kept on the device. These local saves are not sent to the developer's save server.

Banner ads and consent choices

The released Delivery Express and the Android app being prepared for Alchemist display Google AdMob banner ads only and request only non-personalized ads. Google may process advertising identifiers, device and IP information, and ad interactions to provide these ads. Where consent is required, a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) form is shown, and ads load only after ad requests are permitted.

In the game, open Settings → About → Ad privacy choices to review or change your ad consent. This entry appears when privacy options are required.

Kidori: Mosquito Coil Alchemy

Privacy policy — Kidori: Mosquito Coil Alchemy

Information stored on your device

Progress, sound settings, and language are stored only on your device, with no game account or login. The Android app uses SaveStore, SharedPreferences, and WebView localStorage. Preservation copies of damaged or unsupported saves may also remain on the device. There is no developer-hosted cloud save or cross-device progress transfer.

Banner ads and consent choices

The released Delivery Express and the Android app being prepared for Alchemist display Google AdMob banner ads only and request only non-personalized ads. Google may process advertising identifiers, device and IP information, and ad interactions to provide these ads. Where consent is required, a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) form is shown, and ads load only after ad requests are permitted.

Alchemist shows its banner only on title screens with enough room and on menu or journal screens opened from the title. It is not shown during gameplay, while a confirmation dialog is open, or in wide landscape layouts.

In the game, open the ≡ menu → Ad privacy choices to review or change your ad consent. This entry appears when privacy options are required.

Delete or reset your Android advertising ID

Look for ‘Advertising ID’ or ‘Ads’ in Android settings to delete or reset your advertising ID. The location and option names vary by Android version and device manufacturer. Deleting the ID does not erase all advertising information Google already holds; consult Google's guidance as well.
